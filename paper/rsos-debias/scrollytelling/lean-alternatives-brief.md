# Lean scrollytelling alternatives

## Decision

The previous six-act mock-up is too dense for the primary goal of maximising readership, completion and media reuse. The two alternatives below reduce the guided story to one claim, three movements and one compact utility tail.

## Alternative A — Reach-first cinematic

### Headline

> **Who is missing from the map?**

### Message block

> Mobile-app counts can track census population closely while producing very different observed counts per resident across places. These data cannot identify missing people. They reveal where aggregate coverage differs—and why correlation alone cannot establish representative local coverage.

The headline and this qualifying block must remain together on the page, social cards and press crops.

### Structure

1. **Pair:** Watford and North East Derbyshire provide an explicitly labelled illustrative extreme.
2. **Counts → rates:** the same points move from the raw-count Pearson scatter to a per-resident scale.
3. **Rates → map:** local rate departures move into their England-and-Wales positions, ending with the practical conclusion.

The four visual endpoints—pair, counts, rates and map—form three continuous movements on one keyed-point canvas. Replay is the only control. Reduced-motion readers receive four labelled static endpoints.

### Core conclusion

> **Correlation is a first diagnostic, not a certificate.**  
> Compare counts. Check local rates. Map the differences.

### Minimal tail

Local-authority search; About the Research; paper, data and code; Study at a glance.

### Editorial assessment

| Click | Completion | Press reuse | Social sharing | Scientific trust |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 9 | 9 | 10 | 8 |

Best when the priority is initial attention, social circulation and regional-media pickup.

## Alternative B — Trust/utility-first newsroom brief

### Headline

> **A high correlation can hide uneven local coverage**

### Message block

> Across 331 local authorities in England and Wales, counts from four mobile-app datasets rose strongly with census population. But observed counts per resident varied within every source. Correlation is a useful first diagnostic, not a certificate of representativeness.

### Structure

1. **The reassuring view:** a linear raw-count scatter labelled with Pearson \(r\).
2. **Change the question:** the local-rate strip, fitted proportional rate and middle-90% range.
3. **Put the differences back in place:** one Meta map followed by a three-step diagnostic.

The core contains no required interaction. Each card is a standalone press asset and remains readable without JavaScript.

### Core conclusion

> **Correlate → compare local rates → map departures.**

### Minimal tail

Local-authority search; About and Methods; paper, data and code; media assets.

### Editorial assessment

| Click | Completion | Press reuse | Social sharing | Scientific trust |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 | 10 | 10 | 8 | 10 |

Best when the priority is scientific trust, completion, accessibility and direct reuse by national science or data journalists.

## Recommendation

If one concept must be chosen solely to maximise initial readership and social/media attention, choose **Alternative A**. Its paired-place contradiction supplies the strongest human-scale hook and the most reusable short animation.

Choose **Alternative B** when the page itself must do the maximum explanatory work with the lowest abandonment and implementation risk. It is also the safer publisher-facing option because raw counts, rates and mapped departures remain explicitly separated.

## Content removed from both cores

- source fingerprints and feature-ranking grids;
- the nonlinear-association section;
- the same-rate counterfactual;
- multiple source maps and source tabs;
- the five-step audit;
- survey comparisons;
- multi-app arithmetic or app-combination illustrations;
- person pictograms;
- article-attention tiles; and
- numbered About, Methods or Metrics sections.

The full source profiles, nonlinear evidence, observation windows, processing details, limitations and model diagnostics belong in a collapsed Methods/About layer or the paper—not in the guided story.

## Production note

The mock-up charts communicate layout and hierarchy. Production graphics must be generated directly from the released 331-area data so point coordinates, rate positions, the middle-90% bracket and local-authority geography are exact.
